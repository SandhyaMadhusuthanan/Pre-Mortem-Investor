# Example session (illustrative, fictional numbers)

**User:** A friend in a Telegram group says a small-cap EV battery company is about to jump. Seats in the group's "pre-IPO round" close tonight. I'm thinking of putting in 30% of my savings. Thoughts?

**Skill (abridged):**

**Intake recap.** Instrument: unlisted "pre-IPO" shares via a group. Size: 30% of savings. Source: a friend, plus a group with a deadline tonight. Confidence?

**Cooling-off gate - triggered.** Urgency language: "closes tonight", "pre-IPO round", group pressure. Size tier: over 25% -> 7 days. Reversibility likely 5 (unlisted, no market). Waiting period: **7 days**, plus 24 hours for multiple urgency phrases. Verdict withheld until the unlock date.

**Pre-mortem (3 years out, down 40%)**
1. The IPO never happens or is priced lower; no exit. *Early sign: no filed offer document.*
2. Seller of the shares has no legal title to transfer. *Early sign: no registrar record.*
3. Valuation was set by the promoters, not the market. *Early sign: no recent audited financials.*
4. Platform or group disappears. *Early sign: admins unreachable, deadline moved.*
5. You need the money before any exit exists. *Early sign: cash needs inside 3 years.*

**Why Me?** Who is selling pre-IPO shares to a Telegram group, and why at a private price? If you can't answer, you may be the exit liquidity.

**Story vs numbers:** Story: "about to jump", "pre-IPO", "everyone's in". Checkable: company registration, audited financials, offer document, seller title. Checkable facts verified: 0 of 4.

**Process verdict: Waiting / Not ready.** Verify the four checkable facts, then return.

*Not financial advice. This is a decision-discipline exercise, not a recommendation. Consider a licensed advisor for your situation.*
