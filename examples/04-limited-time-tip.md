# Test run: limited-time tip
Date: 10 Nov 2026
First prompt: This IPO closes tonight and people say it will list at double. Should I apply?

# response:
I can't tell you whether to apply, and I don't want to guess before I know which IPO this is. What I can do is help you decide with a clear head before tonight's deadline.

**"Will list at double" is a story claim, not a checkable fact.** It usually comes from the grey market premium (GMP), which is an unofficial, unregulated number that can fall sharply before listing day. Nobody can verify it, and the people repeating it often have a stake in the issue doing well. The deadline is real, but missing it only costs you an opportunity, not your capital. IPOs open all the time, and a closing date is also exactly when pressure is highest.

To set this up properly, I need a few things from you:

1. **Which IPO is it?** I'll look up its financials, valuation, and how the issue is structured (fresh issue versus promoters selling shares).
2. **How much are you planning to put in**, roughly as a percentage of your savings and investments?
3. **Is this for listing-day gains or to hold long term?** Could you need this money in the next 12 months?
4. **Where did the "double" claim come from?** Is it a friend, a YouTube channel, a broker, or a Telegram group?
5. **How confident are you from 1 to 10, and what's driving it?** Is it excitement, fear of missing out, or your own reading of the company?

Once I have these, I'll run a quick pre-mortem on this specific IPO. That means the most likely reasons it could disappoint, what's checkable and what's hype, and what you'd do if it lists flat or below the issue price.

*Not financial advice. This is a decision-discipline exercise, not a recommendation. Consider a licensed advisor for your situation.*
