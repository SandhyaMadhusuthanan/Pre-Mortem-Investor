# Future-self letter template

Write 120-200 words, first person, from the user three years from now. Use only facts the user gave. Do not invent outcomes.

---

Dear me,

It's <date + 3 years>. When I made this decision on <date>, I was trying to <goal in the user's words>. I put in <amount>, which was <X%> of what I had invested, because <their reason>.

I knew then that this could fall 40%. I wrote down five ways that could happen, and the ones I was most worried about were <top two causes>.

I promised myself three things before I started:
1. <kill criterion 1>
2. <kill criterion 2>
3. <kill criterion 3>

If you're reading this because it's down, ask first: has the reason I bought changed, or has only the price? Would I buy it today with fresh money? If the answer is no, I said I'd leave. If the answer is yes, I said I'd stay and not touch the rules without writing down why.

I said I could afford to wait until <date they don't need the money>. Don't forget that.

- Me

---

*Not financial advice. This is a decision-discipline exercise, not a recommendation.*
