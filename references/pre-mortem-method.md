# Pre-mortem method and cause prompts

A pre-mortem works because it is easier to explain a failure that "already happened" than to predict one. Give causes **specific enough that they could be checked next quarter**.

## Format for each cause

| # | Cause | Early warning sign | Within your control? |
|---|-------|--------------------|----------------------|

## Cause categories (pick a mix of at least 4)

1. **Asset-specific**: the business, fund, project or lender itself fails to deliver.
2. **Valuation**: it was fine, but you paid too much; multiples compress.
3. **Structural / liquidity**: gates, lock-ins, thin trading, redemptions frozen.
4. **Counterparty / regulatory / legal**: promoter, platform, developer, custodian, rule changes.
5. **Personal**: you need the cash early, income drops, you panic-sell, you add leverage.

## Prompts by asset class

**Single stock**: earnings miss or guidance cut; customer or supplier concentration; debt maturity wall; promoter pledging or insider selling; governance or accounting issues; sector disruption; dilution; valuation reset.

**Mutual fund / ETF**: fund manager change; style drift; top-heavy holdings; high expense ratio eating returns; tracking error; closing or merging; redemption pressure in a small fund; hidden overlap with what you already own.

**Crypto / tokens**: insider unlock schedules; exchange or custody failure; regulation or ban; smart-contract exploit; liquidity vanishes; team abandonment; stablecoin de-peg.

**Property / deposit / off-plan**: construction delay; developer insolvency; unclear title; approvals missing; rental yield below holding cost; illiquidity; price below purchase when you need to sell; forfeited deposit.

**Loan (taking one)**: floating-rate increase; income shock; prepayment penalties; EMI-to-income too high; collateral risk; refinancing not available when needed.

**Fixed deposit / lock-in products**: premature-withdrawal penalty; inflation beats the rate; issuer risk and insurance limits; reinvestment risk at maturity; tax drag.

**Private deal / friend's business / "guaranteed returns"**: no audited numbers; no exit; key-person risk; fraud; informal paperwork; relationship damage if it fails.

## Quality rules

- Never write a cause that fits every asset ("markets fall"). If it does, rewrite it.
- Rank by likelihood, not by drama.
- At least one cause must be about the **user's own behaviour or cash needs**.
- Pair each cause with something they can monitor.
