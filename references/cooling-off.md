# Cooling-off gate

## Urgency and pressure language to detect
"limited time", "last few units", "price going up tomorrow", "closing today", "only for you", "everyone is buying", "don't miss out", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "once in a lifetime", countdown timers, group chats pushing a decision, a salesperson who will "hold the slot".

Also detect **internal** urgency: "I need to get in before it runs", "I feel stupid for not buying earlier".

## Waiting period

Pick the longer of the size tier and the reversibility tier.

| Size (% of investable assets) | Minimum wait |
|---|---|
| Under 2% | None, or until the checklist is done |
| 2-10% | 24 hours |
| 10-25% | 72 hours |
| Over 25%, or any leverage | 7 days |

| Reversibility score | Minimum wait |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | none |
| 3 | 24 hours |
| 4 | 72 hours |
| 5 | 7 days; for property or large loans, 14 days |

Add **+24 hours** if two or more urgency phrases were found. Cap at 14 days unless the user asks for longer.

These are defaults. State the length and the reason; the user may shorten it **once**, with the reason recorded in the journal.

## While waiting
- Withhold the process verdict. Say: "Structure complete; verdict withheld until [date]."
- Record the unlock date.
- Suggest a few things to do in the gap: verify the Unverified facts, talk to someone with no stake, find a counter-argument, check whether the "deadline" is real.

## On return
1. What changed?
2. Is the deadline still real? Evidence?
3. State the thesis in your own words, without notes.
4. Re-run Story vs Numbers and kill criteria.
5. Then give the process verdict.

## Honest limitation
The skill can't stop the user from acting. It records a commitment the user agreed to and checks it when they come back.
