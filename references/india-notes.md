FD premature withdrawal
Under the RBI's directions for commercial banks, term deposits from individuals of ₹1 crore and below must have a premature withdrawal facility (para 10(3)).
Banks may charge a penalty, but only under a policy approved by their board (para 22). RBI does not set the amount, so it varies by bank. Check your own bank's FD terms.
The penalty components must be told to the depositor when the deposit is opened. If they were not, no penalty may be charged (para 23).
If you withdraw early, interest is paid at the rate for the period the money actually stayed, not the rate you were promised (para 11(1)). No interest is paid if you withdraw before the minimum tenor of seven days (para 11(2) and 10(1)).

Source: RBI (Commercial Banks – Interest Rate on Deposits) Directions, 2025 (dated 28 Nov 2025, updated as on 1 Oct 2026). Last checked: 10 Nov 2026. Verify current rules.

Mutual fund exit loads
An exit load is a charge when you redeem units within the period set in the scheme's offer document. The rate and period differ by scheme, so read that scheme's documents.
A news report says SEBI has cut the maximum exit load from 5% to 3% under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026. The report does not give an effective date, and I have not confirmed this on SEBI's own site.

Lock-ins (ELSS, PPF)
PPF
The account matures after fifteen complete financial years from the end of the year in which it was opened. It can be extended in blocks of 5 years.
Partial withdrawal is allowed once a year from the 7th financial year (that is, after five years from the end of the year of opening). The limit is 50% of the balance at the end of the fourth year before the withdrawal year or the preceding year, whichever is lower (PPF Scheme 2019, para 10).
Premature closure is not allowed before five years, and only for listed reasons: treatment of a life-threatening illness, higher education, or a change in residency status. Interest is then 1% lower (para 13).
A loan can be taken after one year but before five years, up to 25% of the balance at the end of the second preceding year (para 8).

Sources: NSI, Public Provident Fund and India Post, PPF Scheme 2019. Last checked: 10 Nov 2026. Verify current rules.

Property and RERA
Projects above 500 square metres, or with more than 8 apartments, must be registered with the state RERA authority before launch.
Developers must keep 70% of the money collected from buyers in a separate bank account for that project. Withdrawals need certification from an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant.
If the developer misses the completion date in the agreement, the buyer can ask for a refund with interest, or interest for each month of delay until possession.
Rules are also set by each state, so check your state's RERA website to confirm a project's registration before paying anything.
